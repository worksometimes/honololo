# Code of Conduct

HONOLOLO is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically rigorous community.

Participants must:

- be respectful of different backgrounds, experience levels, and opinions;
- give and receive actionable feedback in good faith;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence rather than people;
- protect private information, credentials, and user data.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, deliberate exposure of private data, and
disruptive bad-faith behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove comments, reject changes,
or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and constructive.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer rather than in a public issue.
